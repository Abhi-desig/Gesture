    The room is learned as you move, never including you. Capture the room starts it over and talks you through it: lean far left, then far right (or step out of view), so it sees what you were covering. What it never sees is guessed row by row from the room either side.

    The dust

    Snap practice

    About a minute: snap when you hear the chime, ten times, then move your hand for 20 s without snapping. It measures how many snaps are caught and how often one is seen that wasn't there. Saved on this Mac in experiments/.

    This browser

    The face shows Gesture's state at a glance: asleep when disarmed, watching your hand, a nod when a gesture works, a shrug when it doesn't. Its eyes look toward the screen you're facing. The desktop app (npm run app:dev) also puts it in the orb under your camera, whatever this switch says.
